2. Marks Required for 99 Percentile by Shift Difficulty

Because NTA conducts JEE Main across multiple sessions with varying difficulty, the marks needed for 99 percentile fluctuate significantly:

HARD SHIFT

Tough Physics / Maths

Calculative questions, lengthier algebra and tricky electrodynamics.

  • 99.5 %ile: 180 – 190 Marks
  • 99.0 %ile: 160 – 172 Marks
  • 98.0 %ile: 145 – 155 Marks
  • 95.0 %ile: 118 – 128 Marks
MODERATE SHIFT

Balanced NTA Standard

Formula-based physics, standard NCERT chemistry, moderate maths.

  • 99.5 %ile: 205 – 218 Marks
  • 99.0 %ile: 185 – 195 Marks
  • 98.0 %ile: 165 – 175 Marks
  • 95.0 %ile: 138 – 148 Marks
EASY SHIFT

High Scoring Session

Direct substitution questions and high score cluster at top.

  • 99.5 %ile: 225 – 238 Marks
  • 99.0 %ile: 205 – 216 Marks
  • 98.0 %ile: 185 – 195 Marks
  • 95.0 %ile: 155 – 165 Marks

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What marks are required for 99 percentile in JEE Main 2026?

In a moderate shift, you need between 185 and 195 marks out of 300 to secure 99 percentile. For tougher shifts, 165 marks can suffice, whereas easier shifts may require 210+ marks.

How does Home State (HS) Quota help in NIT admissions?

50% of seats in every NIT are reserved for students from that respective state. In states with fewer high scorers, HS closing ranks can be 10,000 to 25,000 ranks more relaxed compared to the Other State (OS) quota.

Can I get a good NIT with 95 percentile?

Yes! At 95 percentile (approx. CRL Rank 70,000), you can secure core engineering branches (Civil, Chemical, Metallurgy) in established NITs or CSE/IT in prominent IIITs and GFTIs (such as BIT Mesra, Assam University).
