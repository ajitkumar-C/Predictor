Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the closing rank for AIIMS New Delhi?

General category closing rank for AIIMS Delhi typically closes at AIR 57, requiring a score of 710+ out of 720. For OBC candidates, closing rank is around AIR 255.

Do AIIMS institutes have state domicile reservation?

No. All seats across all 25 AIIMS institutes are 100% All India Quota (AIQ) open to students from every state with zero domicile preference.

Is there a rural service bond in AIIMS?

Unlike most State Government Medical Colleges which enforce 1 to 5 years of mandatory rural service bonds, AIIMS MBBS graduates currently have no state service bond requirements.
