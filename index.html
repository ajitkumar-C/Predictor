    College Cutoff Predictor 2026 — Frequently Asked Questions

    Expert answers to the most common questions regarding JoSAA, CSAB, and MCC counseling cutoff trends, rank vs percentile calculations, and college choice optimization.

    A Cutoff Predictor is an algorithmic admission tool that analyzes multi-year opening and closing ranks from official JoSAA, CSAB, and MCC counseling archives. MargDarshak correlates your All India Rank (AIR), category (General, OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST, PwD), and domicile against actual round-by-round seat allocation data to classify your chances into High Chance (Safe), Target (Competitive), and Ambitious (Reach) tiers.
    In government medical colleges across India, 15% of MBBS/BDS seats are contributed to the Medical Counseling Committee (MCC) All India Quota, where candidates from any state can compete purely on merit. The remaining 85% state quota seats are reserved strictly for candidates possessing state domicile, conducted via respective state DME portals (e.g. Maharashtra CET Cell, UP DGME, KEA Karnataka, DME Tamil Nadu). All AIIMS, JIPMER, and Central Universities have 100% All India Quota without domicile restrictions.
    For National Institutes of Technology (NITs), 50% of the total seats are reserved for Home State (HS) candidates who completed their Class 12 from that specific state. The other 50% seats are Other State (OS) open to applicants nationwide. Often, Home State cutoffs are significantly more accessible than Other State cutoffs. In contrast, All 23 IITs allocate seats exclusively on an All India Quota (AI) basis with zero state reservation.
    Your approximate Common Rank List (CRL) rank in JEE Main can be calculated using the formula: Estimated Rank = ((100 - Percentile) / 100) × Total Candidates (approx. 14.2 lakh candidates in 2024-2025). You can use MargDarshak’s built-in Marks vs Rank Calculator (click the top toolbar button) to view instant conversions, percentile benchmarks, and target colleges for both JEE Main and NEET UG.
    Expert counselors recommend the 20-50-30 Choice Filling Rule:
    • Top 20% Choices: Ambitious/Dream colleges (top IITs/AIIMS) that you desire if cutoffs swing in later rounds.
    • Middle 50% Choices: Target colleges where your rank is within 5-10% of last year's closing rank.
    • Bottom 30% Choices: Safe/High Chance colleges to guarantee you secure a verified seat without risk of disqualification.
    Most commercial college predictor websites force students to enter their mobile numbers to capture leads, resulting in relentless telemarketing spam and aggressive promotion of low-grade private colleges. MargDarshak is engineered as an open, public-benefit educational resource: 100% free, zero login wall, zero spam calls, and 100% verified official data directly from JoSAA and MCC counseling authorities.

      How MargDarshak Works & Counseling Guide

      Understand how predictions are calculated & how to fill your counseling choices

      1. Official Verified Data

      • NEET UG & PG: Extracted from official MCC (mcc.nic.in) Round 1, 2, 3 and Stray Vacancy seat allotment lists.
      • JEE Advanced (IITs): Official JoSAA (josaa.nic.in) Opening & Closing Rank archives for all 23 IITs.
      • JEE Main (NITs/IIITs): Official JoSAA + CSAB Special Round cutoffs with 50% Home State (HS) vs 50% Other State (OS) logic.

      2. Probability Tier Methodology

      • 🟢 High Chance (Safe): Your rank is comfortably better than the cutoff (within 85% of closing rank). Safe to secure in early rounds.
      • 🟡 Target (Moderate): Borderline cutoff range. Highly competitive; keep in your top 10 choices for Round 2 and 3.
      • 🔴 Ambitious (Reach): Higher cutoff than current rank. Add these at the top for seat-upgradation and stray rounds.

      3. Smart Choice-Filling Strategy

      • Top 20% Choices: Add Ambitious dream colleges. There is no penalty for aspirational choices in JoSAA or MCC.
      • Middle 50% Choices: Add Target colleges where your rank is close to the 3-year cutoff.
      • Bottom 30% Choices: Add Safe colleges to guarantee you do not exit counseling without a seat.

      4. 100% Free & Spam-Free

      • No Phone Numbers Required: We never ask for your mobile number or sell your personal details to private colleges.
      • Export Preference List: Build your priority sheet using the ⭐ bookmark button and export or print it for free.
      • Real-Time Filters: Filter by Category (EWS, OBC, SC, ST, PwD), Domicile, Gender, and Branch.
