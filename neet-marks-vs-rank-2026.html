Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What rank will I get with 650 marks in NEET UG 2026?

With 650 marks, your expected All India Rank (AIR) is estimated between 6,500 and 7,500. This provides a very high probability of securing an MBBS seat in top State Government Medical Colleges under 15% AIQ and 85% State Quota.

What is the minimum safe score for a Government Medical College (GMC)?

For General/UR candidates, a safe score under 15% All India Quota is 615+ marks (AIR under 23,000). Under 85% State Quota, safe scores vary between 570 and 610 marks depending on your state domicile.
